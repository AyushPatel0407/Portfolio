For the last three years I've spent most of my time building software for businesses that run on spreadsheets, phone calls and WhatsApp groups: a manufacturing ERP, a B2B chemicals marketplace, a shared WhatsApp inbox, an industrial pump store and an event ticketing site.

Every one of those projects taught me something I wish I'd known at the start. Until now, those lessons have mostly lived in my head and in old pull requests. This blog is where I'll start writing them down.

## What I'll write about

- **Building business software for Indian SMEs:** GST e-invoicing, inventory, production planning and the messy reality of replacing Tally plus Excel.
- **The MERN stack and Next.js in production:** architecture decisions, performance, and the mistakes that cost the most time.
- **Integrations:** WhatsApp Business, Razorpay, OTP sign-in and the other pieces that make products feel finished.
- **Leading a small team:** what changed when I moved from writing all the code to helping a team ship it.

## Why write at all?

Writing forces me to understand things properly. If a post helps one other developer avoid a mistake I made, that's a bonus.
